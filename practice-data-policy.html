Practice Data & Market Source Policy

Project: THE STOCK MARKET: FROM THE GROUND UP (Crownlight) · Version 1.1 · Updated November 4, 2026 · Status: Public educational source disclosure

Current baseline: Unless an individual tool explicitly identifies a verified source, instrument, market session and observation time, its chart prices, volume, transactions and indicator examples are simulated educational data, not live exchange quotations or verified historical trades.

1. Simulated practice charts and lessons

Training candles, evolving OHLCV, replay sequences, technical indicators, market-structure annotations and worked transactions may be generated or authored to teach a specific concept. They illustrate mathematical methods and decision processes. Do not use these values as executable market prices, brokerage instructions or evidence that a real instrument traded at the displayed values. A bar-volume VWAP approximation is not the same as trade-print VWAP.

2. Verified historical and member-supplied information

A feature using historical or member-supplied data should identify its source, applicable symbol, market session, as-of time, and any delay or usage limitation. Historical prices are not necessarily available in real time. Member-supplied feeds, imports or keys are governed by the source provider's rights and relevant product access rules. Crownlight does not promise redistribution rights for licensed third-party feeds.

3. Member statistics and demonstration configurations

“Online members” refers to a recent-activity measure, and “Registered members” refers to account registrations, only when the widget identifies the source as LIVE DATA. An “EXAMPLE” badge identifies Owner-configured demonstration figures. Such displayed figures and percentages are not verified membership totals, even when the metric labels are simply Online members and Registered members.

4. Transition to verified market sources

When verified market data becomes available for a particular chart, that chart must disclose its actual provenance, time basis and source-specific limitations. Other practice charts remain simulated until separately verified. A policy statement alone does not turn generated observations into verified real-world data.

5. Visible simulation disclosures

Throughout the website, SIMULATION, Simulated, or Demonstration identifies educationally generated market examples. The absence of those words from an individual axis or sentence does not make that information live or historical. Every chart and tool remains covered by the Current baseline above unless that specific interface identifies a verified data provider, symbol, session, and observation time.

For membership metrics, the normal labels Online members and Registered members do not by themselves verify figures. The EXAMPLE badge means the Owner selected fixed or generated demonstration counts; LIVE DATA means a verified aggregate, subject to the metric's stated measurement period. The percentage gauge uses no secondary source caption in EXAMPLE mode; the EXAMPLE badge and this policy establish its provenance.

6. Education and financial decisions

Lessons, chart examples, tutor explanations and indicator demonstrations are educational, not investment advice or guarantees of future price behavior. Users should independently verify market information through a properly licensed source before making financial decisions.

See also the Educational & Trading Disclaimer, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy. This source disclosure supplements, and does not silently amend or supersede, existing contractual terms.
