## Controlling method decisions

1. **Input identity:** Every case declares price/volume/quote fields, currency or units, instrument/universe, eligible time cutoff, session, chart timeframe, lookback and missing bars. A quote is not a trade, and a completed candle cannot be inferred while it is forming.
2. **Formula versioning:** The original 73 guide formulas are preserved and identified. Where the source only describes an algorithm family, teach conceptually unless the specified exact variant and worked case are independently reproducible.
3. **Warmup and seed:** EMA uses the existing project SMA seed with alpha=2/(N+1); Wilder indicators require their own defined average/recursive seed; nested smoothing waits for the complete chain. No zero padding or fabricated initial outputs.
4. **VWAP:** Exact trade-print numerator/denominator and HLC3 bar-volume proxy are distinct methods. In the existing shared seven-method Lab fixture, trade VWAP=11.50 and HLC3 proxy=11.333333 for the same fictional transactions/bar. The discrepancy is expected, not a rounding defect. TradingView's official bar-proxy method and thinkorswim's reference remain candidate methodology sources.
5. **RSI/ATR/MACD/Bollinger:** Preserve individually documented seeding/warmup. In the existing fictional Lab method: Wilder RSI handles both-zero gain/loss as project-defined 50, ATR starts from a defined first true range, MACD waits for the signal EMA, and Bollinger dispersion uses the population divisor N.
6. **Recursive complexity:** KAMA requires efficiency ratio and squared adaptive smoothing; KST requires four differently smoothed ROC legs and disclosed weights; ADX requires Wilder +DM/-DM/TR/DI/DX and an initial ADX mean; TRIX needs triple-EMA warmup and a final ROC. Individual parameter variants must be stated, not silently assumed. Exact per-study specifications are in the machine-readable register.
7. **Portfolio and breadth context:** Price-relative, market breadth, option-volume and SCTR measurements require time-aligned source/universe membership and denominator policy; fictional OHLC bars alone cannot justify an official cross-sectional rank.
8. **Rights and AI:** Every source link is a research citation only until independently assessed for copying, training/automated AI processing, chart images, code, market-data display and trademark use.
