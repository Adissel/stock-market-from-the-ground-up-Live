## Execution scope

Six independent mathematical methods, ten source-original fictional cases per method: candle anatomy; completed-close SMA(3); weighted HLC3 **bar VWAP proxy** (not trade-print VWAP); hypothetical fixed risk budget and planned slippage reserve; confirmed-bar range high comparison; and nonoverlapping three-minute aggregation from completed fictional one-minute bars.

The **60** records are generated deterministically from original synthetic OHLCV and never fetch market data. Each is available in the staff-only authenticated Prototype Center under a new expanding section. Keyboard-operable selection, previous/next completed observation, reset, a tabular OHLCV representation, and on-demand method disclosure are implemented. Partial-data cases explicitly indicate insufficient observations. Neither numeric method nor display forecasts prices.

## Genuine acceptance distinction

- Automated tests verify 60 unique cases, six method groups, mathematical source values, every no-future as-of prefix, source gating and no grading/billing/member-data calls. This is not the same as 60 individually approved official Lab lessons.
- Static script file may load while the staff gate is closed, but the visual workbench cannot mount unless the existing `pt-app` is authorized and unhidden. This does not replace server-side role authorization.
- This change is intentionally separate from in-flight PR #40's 57 member-facing examples and seven legal disclosures. No overlap with 419 immutable canonical lessons, release flags, member data, receipts, grades, Lumens or tier entitlements.
- Prior independent 419 lesson source and premium acceptance gates remain HOLD. The staff preview does not increase 16/64 integrated feature counts.
