## Subject-specific acceptance boundaries

- **Range and overlap:** high−low arithmetic, common intersection of bar ranges and the distinction between shrinking volatility and directional certainty.
- **Timeframe evidence:** one-, three- and five-minute completion boundaries, lookback window versus candle interval, honest partial-day aggregation.
- **Completed-scale interpretation:** conflicting green/red candles on different timeframes, and identical OHLC with different possible intrabar paths.
- **Market structure:** broadly upward/downward/non-directional completed paths; unconfirmed swing look-ahead; inward/outward contradictory high-low comparisons.
- **Support and resistance:** tentative reaction zones, spread/zone arithmetic, and no hindsight after a later support break.

Each revised Lab contains three original subject-specific paragraphs in each of five instructional facets (Understand/Recognize/Apply/Common Mistake/Limitations), original self-check language, and a distinct new fictional ungraded transfer exercise. Protected answer keys and official grades remain server-only.
