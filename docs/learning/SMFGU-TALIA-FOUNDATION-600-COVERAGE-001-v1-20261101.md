## Product-owner decision: 600 Tutor explanation topics, not 600 new official graded competencies
The **30 existing official competency identifiers remain unchanged** for Skills, protected grading, and Lab progress. The 600 requirement is a *new Tutor instructional-explanation editorial inventory*. A Lab, Boot Camp day, indicator variant or source link may inspire several genuinely different teaching subjects, but it does not earn approval merely because its identifier exists.

Each accepted explanation requires its own distinct concept boundary, substantive first-principles teaching, accurately sourced input data and units, calculation or justified no-calculation declaration, fully worked fictional illustration when appropriate, correct/incorrect interpretation, precise limitation or invalidation, beginner misconception correction, fresh independent practice, transfer prompt and explicit no-grade/no-advice/no-future-evidence restrictions. Editorial reviewers must confirm each item is not a paraphrase or duplicate of another. A researched topic, generated inventory entry or formula name alone is **not** accepted.
