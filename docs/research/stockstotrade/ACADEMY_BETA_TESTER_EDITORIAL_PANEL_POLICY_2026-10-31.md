**Purpose:** Implement the Owner's decision to recruit *independent human editorial reviewers from real beta testers*, rather than requiring a separate external editorial company.

## 1. Owner-approved decision and boundary

**Beta testers are the human editorial review cohort.** There is no requirement to hire a separate editor merely to obtain a human viewpoint. However, *beta tester* is not synonymous with *independent editor*: a tester's feedback is attributable evidence, and becomes an independently attributable editorial decision **only when the participant is eligible for the exact assigned review task, actually examines the relevant material, records the evidence and makes an explicit decision.**

The publisher retains accountability for final editorial acceptance. Owner approval of a checklist does not substitute for a human tester's actual reading, source check, or assessment inspection. No synthetic QA identity, automated GPT-6 judgment or script may impersonate a human reviewer.

The controlled **ungraded** B01 beta continues while these decisions are collected. **Official grading, mastery claims and certification dependent on protected assessment remain HOLD** until complete accepted evidence and real member QA.

## 2. Two reviewer tracks within the beta tester cohort

| Track | Beta tester eligibility / task | May inspect protected keys? | Editorial authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Learning & factual reviewer** | Real authenticated eligible beta member; actively consents to review; independently reads assigned instructional day, checks clarity, answers unguided transfer and reports precise factual/source concerns | **NO** | Supplies attributable learning and factual observations and evidence, including ACCEPT / REVISE recommendation |
| **B. Confidential assessment reviewer** | An expressly selected, verified, competent beta member who signs confidentiality/independence acknowledgment, passes a reviewer orientation, and is assigned a specific private set | **Only the assigned set, in a future separately authorized, least-privilege reviewer interface** | Independently checks every question/key/distractor/numeric tolerance/feedback for the assigned set; may give case-level APPROVE / REVISE decisions |

**Separation of duties:** A tester assigned to private set `S` and exposed to its answers is **permanently disqualified from being an unseen/blind competency tester for S or any materially equivalent version**. For each protected set, obtain one Track B assessment reviewer and a **different**, answer-blind beta tester for usability and independent attempt. A member may be on both tracks for *different* units if conflicts are documented. Do not reveal answer keys to ordinary beta members or authorize assessment access merely because they have purchased a membership.

A beginner can validly review instructional clarity but should not be presented as a qualified reviewer of securities regulations, market data licenses or broker-specific compliance. For source-sensitive claims, independently corroborate current effective-dated authority and assign a reviewer with demonstrated relevant subject knowledge. A tester's confidence alone is not sufficient evidence.

## 3. Qualifying and recruiting testers

- No automatic opt-in, account promotion, role elevation, special payment, Lumens award or additional tool entitlement. Participation is voluntary and separate from paid membership benefits.
- Require the member to affirm their real authenticated account, the assignment scope, confidentiality rules (if Track B), source-citation expectations and conflicts of interest. Obtain express notice of how personal identifiers and submissions will be used. No blanket legal enforceability claim is made by this policy.
- The Owner selects and assigns beta reviewers from actual volunteers, records a stable account UUID (in **private** access-controlled reviewer records only), training/subject-matter suitability, review track, assignment, qualification rationale, effective dates and withdrawal status.
- Assign source verification and teaching review *independently*. Reviewers must not be the AI author masquerading as another identity, and must not simply endorse a prefilled answer without opening the actual item.

## 4. Required review evidence

For each of the **945** current sentence/instruction records: immutable claim location and source revision/hash; classification as externally verifiable claim, fictional calculation or teaching method; either a dated authoritative source citation and exact scoped finding, a fully reproduced arithmetic check, or an observable pedagogy/transfer test; result `ACCEPT` / `REVISE` / `NOT_ENOUGH_EVIDENCE`; explanation, reviewer account ID and UTC timestamp.

For each of **42** day-level packages: all material individual items reconciled; original instruction, objectives, numerical cases, independent transfer case, feedback and hindsight/data-rights caveats inspected. Issue a day-level recommendation only after its constituent findings are complete. Disagreement triggers adjudication and re-review.

For each of **20** private B01 v98 sets: verify **all five choice stems, three choices each, the actual correct key, two wrong-option corrective explanations for each choice, the numerical question/key/units/tolerance and any partial credit**. A reviewer must make an independently reasoned case-level decision rather than toggle a general `review_state`. Private reviewer evidence may reference a question ID and set version but **never embed secret keys in GitHub, email, public web forms or member-visible comments**.

Attributable evidence should have reviewer identity, role, assignment ID, conflict check, version/hash, per-item decision, source/proof, meaningful rationale, UTC timestamp and edit history. Keep the canonical registers controlling; proposed panel decisions must be reconciled with them, never silently replace them.

## 5. Workflow — two distinct acceptance populations

1. **Invite and onboard** beta testers to Track A; optionally shortlist qualified volunteers for Track B. Until separately authorized reviewer permissions and safe interfaces exist, **do not** grant Track B private-question access.
2. **Track A:** assign nonsecret lesson and source-review packets, accept structured observations, correct text or source defects, and ask *another* tester to re-check revised material. This is ungraded feedback, not exam evidence.
3. **Track B:** after least-privilege, audited access is deployed and tested, assign each private B01 set to a qualified reviewer distinct from its blind usability tester; run secure question-by-question review and record individual decisions.
4. **Owner/publisher editorial reconciliation:** verify actual tester evidence and resolve disagreements, source gaps or inadequate qualifications. Record final decisions under `252_instruction_source_field_QA_v2.csv` and `MASTER_671_ROW_EVIDENCE_AND_ACCEPTANCE_v1.csv`, preserving their keys and history. Mark no unit accepted while blocking claims, protected tests or reviewer evidence remain unresolved.
5. **Final release acceptance:** the independent beta test population performs genuine native browser sign-in, consent, entitlement, RLS isolation and graded replay under newly reviewed private sets. Only subsequently consider enabling official grading and mastery. No production money charges without separate express permission.

**Reviewer identity and audit format:** `reviewer_type=AI`; `reviewer_name=GPT-6`; `authority=OWNER_DELEGATED_PUBLISHER_EDITOR`; `independence=NOT_HUMAN_INDEPENDENT`. **Permitted decisions:** GPT-6 may check source facts, historical evidence restrictions, numerical calculations, instructional structure, private case logic, answer-key coherence, wrong-option feedback and accessible workflows, then sign an **individually reasoned AI/publisher editorial decision** such as `AI_EDITORIAL_SUPPORTED`, `AI_EDITORIAL_REVISION_REQUIRED` or `AI_EDITORIAL_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, with linked exact code revision, source authority, test evidence, limitations and UTC date. A decision is never automatic from the permission grant. The Owner's existing frozen v97 and original register decisions remain unchanged without an individual finding and controlled reconciliation.

**Important control distinction:** The Owner may choose AI-assisted publisher editing as the publication workflow, but an AI-only review is not a *human-independent* review. Prior human-beta editorial acceptance gates must therefore either be genuinely met by separate consenting reviewers or explicitly reclassified as **not met / waived by the publisher** in a versioned, plainly disclosed policy decision; their missing results may not be recorded as passes. No such blanket waiver or official protected-grading release is executed by this policy update. Real logged-in beta-member usability, entitlement, confidentiality, segregation, RLS, grading and screen-reader acceptance cannot be simulated as human activity.

**Release rules remain conservative:** B01 version-98 Draft grading and mastery remain HOLD. No promotion of a private question set to reviewed; no answer key disclosure; no confidential Track B access based only on paid membership; no fabricated reviewer UUID or status. AI source-review evidence can advance the **publisher editorial** workstream. Genuine member acceptance and protected scoring tests must still complete before enabling official grading. Human feedback remains welcome for teaching quality even if publisher editorial work is AI-led.
